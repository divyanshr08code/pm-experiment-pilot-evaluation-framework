Difference-in-differences holds in S1 and S4, is slightly biased in S2, and fails in S3 (bias +0.67 min, coverage 52.0%), because a city-level shock hits treated stores more, which breaks parallel trends. Before/after understates the effect, because planted drift pushes delivery times up; it declares a false effect in 36.5% of no-effect runs, against 4.5% for DiD.

These conclusions depend on the simulator's assumptions.

Power

ICC 0.285 and about 70 days per store give a design effect of 20.6. Measured DiD MDE is 0.77 min for 15 stores per arm, against the planning value of 0.735 and 0.75 from replications.
